🏭 SCADA Canvas Library - Drag & Drop Example

📋 Instructions

🖱️ Select: Click on elements to select them

🖱️ Multi-Select: Hold Ctrl/Cmd + click for multiple selection

🖱️ Drag: Click and drag selected elements to move them

🔄 Resize: Click and drag the corner handles of selected elements

🖱️ Pan Canvas: Click and drag on empty space to pan

🖱️ Zoom: Use mouse wheel to zoom in/out

⌨️ Delete: Press Delete or Backspace to remove selected elements

⌨️ Select All: Press Ctrl/Cmd + A to select all elements
